-=> INTRODUCTION <=-
This addon basicly adds the original UR models made by Steki
(www.frag.com/deconstruct) (except for the new wheels for the truck).
I also added sound for the BMW and the Mini which I got from Ridah.
Any one that has proper sounds for any of the other vehicles can
e-mail them to me and I will try to put them in.

The biggest addition is the racing bots.  The bots don't have any health
so they cannot die, they won't show up on the scores, and they don't shoot.
I have placed markers in maps QR1 - QR4.  I tried putting markers in QR5
but the first section was too tight and the bots couldn't navigate.  The
bots are not extreamly good and have some bugs (look at the bottom for a bug
list).


-=> INSTALLATION/USAGE <=-

After downloading the qCR.zip file, unzip it WITH PATH NAMES INTACT into
your /quake/ directory.

-=> NEW IMPULSES AND KEYS <=-

	Impulse 25      'o'		Observer cam mode
	Impulse 150	'['		Goto previous observer target.
	Impulse 151	']'		Goto next observer target.

	Impulse 33			Toggle strafe style tracking.

	Impulse 210			Select Mini with 1st skin
	Impulse 211			Select Volvo with 1st skin
	Impulse 212			Select BMW with 1st skin
	Impulse 213			Select Viper with 1st skin
	Impulse 214			Select Monster Truck with 1st skin
	Impulse 215			Select Jeep with 1st skin
	Impulse 216			Select Hummer with 1st skin

	Impulse 70      'b'		Spawn a bot
	Impulse 71      'r'		Remove all bots


-=> KNOWN BUGS/PROBLEMS <=-

There are some bugs in Quake Rally. Please read this list before mailing
us with any problems.

- Cars are not totally solid (Can't fix this)
- Sometimes bots will bounce when following right behind other cars (Fixed, I think)
- Bots sometimes go through walls or floors when in the air (Fixed, or at least not
	as bad as before)
- Sometimes a bot will continuously circle a marker (Fixed)
- Bots fly into the air sometimes after starting downhill (most often in QR3 map) (Fixed)
- Bots go half way through the floor in one part of QR3 (This is not a problem
	in code.  I think something is wrong with the level in that spot)
- Repulsor models don't fit perfectly on some cars
- Sometimes a bot disappears when the race starts (Fixed, I think)
- Bots hardly move on some maps. (QR4 in particular)
- Quake has been know to crash once in a while when running bots
