Roadrunner by Alex Parker 1998 version 1
(alexp@albany.jrc.net.au)

A quake rally map (find it at impact.frag.com)

A track which takes you from the city, down a back country road,
through underground tunnels and over death - defying lava jumps!

Watch out for the roo's in the outback bit.

Average time - It takes me an average time of 71.5 seconds per lap.
My fastest time is 69.5 seconds - Not bad eh?

Bugs - Well, the tracking camera does pop out of the roof in places
but this can be avoided by keeping the camera angle low :)

Secrets - Find the pentagram of protection.  It's a pretty useless
addition but its there nontheless.

How to get around quicker...
When you go over the final jump, swing the camera around 90 degrees
to the right and the car will change direction mid air and land on
the roof of the apartment block. If you do it just so, you can hack 
a whopping 0.5 seconds off overall time.

OH, one final note, turn on those headlights :)

Cya round, and thanks to IMPACT for such wonderful game (it is a completely
new game!)
