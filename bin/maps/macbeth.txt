			  Macbeth's Backyard
			   Quake Rally Map

   ================================================================

	Filename                : macbeth.bsp
	Release Date            : November 8th, 1997
	Author                  : Karl Waskiewicz

	Email Address           : friendofshawn@hotmail.com


   Possibly the most difficult Quake Rally Map to complete in time
   trial.  Lots of obstacles to avoid, and very little time to do so.

                        
   I think this would be a good moment to say how much I *despise*
   using Worldcraft to make maps.  BSP rules...It was always way
   more advanced and easier to use than whatever else.

    *Update: November 9: Taking shortcuts is much harder and riskier
                         now...don't even try it at more difficult
			 skill levels.
   
   When playing this map, you can expect to get a lap time of between
   50-65 seconds.

   ================================================================


   Map Info summary:
    -Racing with weapons
    -All weapons included somewhere in map
    -5 lap limit
    -Successful Wind push incorporation
    -910 brushes, 650 entities
    -4 deathmatch starts

   ---

   Level is made from scratch using BSP 0.86b (Better than all the rest
   combined).  No bugs are known of.

	qbsp:  10 minutes 36 seconds
	light: About 40 minutes
	vis:   7 1/2 Hours

   Qrally.wad as well as the standard Quake texture set was used.

   Machine compiled on was a P133/32m

   ---

   If you choose to put this map on a cd and call it your own,
   I don't want to know.
