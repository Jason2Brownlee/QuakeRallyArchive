Title                   : Blue Lake Mountain
Filename                : blm.bsp

Author                  : Ben "Krash" Ackland
Email Addresses         : krash@hunterlink.net.au
                          Bug reports, tips, etc. are most welcome.

Description             : A map in which you race around the inside
                          of an extinct volcano. The volcano has
                          developed a cachement of water in its caldera,
                          hence the name. You will be racing through
                          underground rivers, twisting tunnels, and a
                          limestone cave. There are several places where
                          the map splits, and some difficult jumps.
                          I also have a makeshift pitstop.
                          Good Luck!

Best Lap Time           : 47.1 seconds. Set by me.
                          anyone who can manage under 46 seconds,
                          PLEASE SEND ME A DEMO!

Special thanks to       : The Impact Team for QRally
                          Ben Morris for worldcrdaft
