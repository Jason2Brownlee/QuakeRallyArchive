Description             : QuakeRally Track.
                          Racing with Deathmatch implemented.  This map,
                          #1 in my DeathRace series, is a small figure 8
                          with obstacles to avoid and weapons spread through-
                          out.  To win, it will take both speed and good aim!

                          If you wonder why there are soo many checkpoints,
                          it's to keep people on track.  Think - having only 2
                          checkpoints results in the person chopping 75% of
                          the track off...
                           
Other                   : Built and tested on a Packard Bell Pentium 60.
			  Ran extremely smooth, if you have trouble and a
			  faster computer, it's not my map, it's your 
			  computer.  heh.  Your probably laughing.
