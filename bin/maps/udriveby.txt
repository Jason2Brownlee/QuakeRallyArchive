Title                : Urban Driveby
Filename             : udriveby.bsp
Version              : 1.0 [dated:7/31/97]

Description          : This is a new track for QuakeRally.
                       The city textures are so overused it really
                       isn't funny. It's been designed with netplay
                       and 486's in mind, but the ammo in the level
                       is sparse.. watch your aim. No RL either, due
                       to a bug in QuakeRally where the game doesn't
                       accecelrate on certain angled surfaces and you
                       keep a constant speed.. visicous cycle with the
                       RL. Everyone that has played this map though it
                       was uber-good, so something has to be right.

                       Note: this map hasn't really been tested for
                       netplay. I'm probably gonna either make this
                       a pure racing map or fix it up, but the map
                       speed is cool for netplay [r_speeds below 200,
                       there's a sight.

Author               : Steven Towle aka Dipstick
Email Address        : stevet3@ix.netcom.com | stevet@ftp.cdrom.com

Misc. Author Info    : Someone too damn young for his own good.
                       Co-author [with about 20 other people] of
                       Memento Mori 2. Also a level designer on
                       the ManyWorlds project. Yeah, this is my
                       first publically released map for Quake, tough
                       crumbs.
