When the car becomes air-bourne it maintains its previous rolling velocity. So to make the car roll in the air you would place a slanted ramp, or a hump, which causes the car to roll to the side while going up it. When the car becomes air-bourne it simply maintains this roll velocity.

Remember that there must be NO stairs in the track. All surfaces must be slanted and connecting. See the hump in BRIDGES.MAP for an example of how I would like rolls to be induced.
