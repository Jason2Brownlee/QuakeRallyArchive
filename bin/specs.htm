QuakeRally Specs

TRACKS
Get the example Tracks here.. get the latest FAQ here.. then read the following:

Please take note of all of the following pointers BEFORE startinng with your track!

Jumps

Jumps must not be steeper than 30 degrees. See BRIDGES and TUNNELS for an example of maximum steepness.

Track Segments

Track segments must join perfectly. No gaps or unaligned segments.

Visibilty

Pay attention to how much of the track is visible at each part of the track. BRIDGES is a bad example in this regard. If I was going to use this track in the FINAL I would put some sort of cover over the bridges to prevent the player from seeing the whole track.

Entity Placement

We will add the custom rally entities, so for now, only add things like health packs, weapons (no Shotguns) and deathmatch starts. The deathmatch starts are only used at the start of the race. Rally entities are used to respawn players during the race. Therefore Deathmatch starts should be placed much like the strting grid for F1 Grand Prix racing. The cars are 64 units wide, so to be safe place the deathmatch starts at least 96 units apart.

Track Style

I admit it will be tough to make a perfect track without having the game to test with. I'm afraid your just going to have to use your judgement here. I would like to give out betas to you guys, but then whats stopping people from saying "Yeah I'll make some tracks" just to get their greedy hands on a beta. Just try to use the example tracks to help you here. Thre reason these tracks are flat is because I used 3DS to create them and whenever I tried to make hills the segments became all dis-joint. I am hoping this is a problem with the keyscripter, but in any case it wont be easy to make hilly tracks. Just try to make the track windy (lotsa turns) to create the sliding effects we all love in rally driving. Jumps are cool, but make them relatively easy. The jumps in those example tracks hard to complete succesfully, mainly because there is a turn just before them. Try to give the driver a nice straight to get lined up properly.

Also note the hump that is used in BRIDGES. This humps is extremely cool, as it creates lots of in-air rolls and it's fun to watch someone rolling their car :). Try to place some things this around your track, but make sure you use a sphere or something with lots of sides and dont make the base of the hump too steep or it will result in the car jumping over the hump instead of driving over it.

I would REALLY like to see a track with small lumps on the track surface. These would have to be much smaller than the hump mentioned above, so that the car just cruises over it. This would be just to create a more realistic envirnment where the road isnt perfectly flat all the time. Real rally tracks have small bumps all over them, thats what I'm looking for here.

Textures

I have found only 3 textures out of the quake101.wad texture library that work well as road surface textures. These are COP1_1, GRAVE01_1 and GRAVE01_3. If anyone out there is able to create some cool asphalt , mud and dirt textures I would love to hear from you. But otherwise please stick to these textures as the main road surface texture. You may try some others, but just make sure that the textures is seemless and doesnt have a "box" around it.

Lights

Please make good use of lights in your track. I will tend to view you rtrack more favourably if it looks good. Notice the use of the walls for shadow casting onto the track in BRIDGES. This makes the road surface looks much better, IMHO, however try not to make black areas like in some places around BRIDGES. A black wall causes headaches for drivers as it's hard to see them and generally results in excessive car damage :).

Final Note

For those of you who have the KARTSPEC.TXT written by QC_Wedge for the QuakeKarts project, I would advise you to have another read through that. If not then you can get it here. I was just looking at it then and almost everything in there applies to QuakeRally. So instead of re-typing the same stuff, make sure you go now and read through the whole file. Wedge gives some excellent tips for Track Editors in the Track Building DO'S & DONT'S section.

If there is anything else I have missed then go ahead and ask me, but please make this your last option. I have lots of things on my plate right now, and that is the reason I am asking y'all for help with the tracks. I especially dont wanna read 10 emails a day asking questions that you can find the answer by having another read through here, or reading the KARTSPEC'S. Otherwise have fun editing and I hope that you can experience the same thrill I get out of contributing to a cool game like this :).

-Ridah
MODELS
Please take note of all of the following pointers BEFORE startinng with your model!
  • All cars should be have the following dimensions (Width x Length x Height): 48 x 72 x 55
  • The car body (everything but the tyres) should be a single model. This should be centred perfectly by the XY axis.
