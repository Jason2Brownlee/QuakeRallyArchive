### Cancelled (February 17 1998)

Mentioned on BluesNews:

> Ultimate Rally Dead: "Mini" QuakeRally 1.3 Not? [Feb 17, 1998, 11:00 am ET] – Post a Comment
>
> Ridah posted some sad news on the Impact Development Team page, that Ultimate Rally, the planned uber-sequel to QuakeRally (the Quake driving conversion), will not be completed: a victim of the lure of programming Quake II over Quake. There is still hope for QuakeRally fans, however, as Sumaleth posted a subsequent update saying he would see what he could do to package the work that's been done on Ultimate Rally as a "mini" QuakeRally 1.3 release. Thanks rdwarlock.

-- https://www.bluesnews.com/s/230974/ultimate-rally-dead-mini-quakerally-1-3-not

Mentioned on Redwoods:

> February 17, 1998\
> Bye Bye Ultimate Rally\
> D'oh! Word on the Impact page is that work on Ultimate Rally is no more, but Sumaleth will try to get what is there together for a mini 1.3 rally release. That kind of bites, but Quake II is having it's effect on Quake 1 programming I guess. Now we just need a Rally for Quake II. :o) I saw this tidbit on Blue's.

### Release of Ultimate Rally Cars (March 08 1998)

Cars developed for Ultimate Rally were released for Quake Rally v1.2.

Released as:

* `hummer.ZIP`
* `fwhl_oz.ZIP`

Mentioned on BluesNews:

> Quake Mods (Updated) [Mar 08, 1998, 11:00 am ET] – Post a Comment
>
> Used Quake Car Lot
> On Deconstruct is a set of all the cars created for the never completed Ultimate Rally, converted for use with QuakeRally 1.2, the final released version of this classic driving mod for classic Quake (available on the Impact Website). The archive is available in three varieties: GL, non-GL, and original (unscented?) which distorts the cars, but is probably the most useful variety for mod authors who want to use them in there own project (which is permitted).

-- https://www.bluesnews.com/s/231248/quake-mods-updated
